About GHSF

We bring the gear. You bring the story.

Ghost Hunters of South Florida investigate reports of paranormal activity across South Florida. We take every report seriously, keep an open mind, and stay honest about what the evidence does and doesn't show.

How we investigate

We start by listening. Before a visit we talk with you about what's been happening, when, and where.

On site we record audio and video, and take readings throughout the session. Some of our equipment we build ourselves.

Afterward we go back through the recordings and share what we found with you, including the things that turned out to have an ordinary explanation.

What we promise

Respect for your home or business, and for the people connected to it.

Discretion. We don't publish anything from your investigation without your permission.

Straight answers. If we can't explain something, we'll say so, and if we can, we'll say that too.
